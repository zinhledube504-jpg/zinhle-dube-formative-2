ORGANIZATION OVERERVIEW Name: Dube Technologies Background: • Dube Technologies is a small technology-focused organization that provides technology solutions and services to homes, schools, retail stores, and businesses. The organization focuses on helping clients improve safety, security and efficiency through modern technologies such as Internet of Things(iot) devices, smoke detection systems, biometric sensors, network installation and other smart devices.
I propose to develop a professional website for the business in which the website will help establish an online presence for the business, promote its services, and allow clients to learn more about the company and contact it easily. Mission: • To provide reliable and accessible technology solutions that improve safety, security, and efficiency for organizations and individuals. Vision: • For the business to become a trusted technology solutions provider that helps communities and businesses to adapt to new digital tools and make use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Automation in all business areas. Target Audience: • The target audience includes homeowners • School Administrators • Small businesses
• Offices
• Hospitals
• Organizations for IT Solutions.
WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES GOAL: • To create a professional online presence for Dube Technologies and make it easier for customers to understand and access its services. • Help people move from old ways of doing things into making them digitaly OBJECTIVES: • KPIs such as website visits, contact forms, enquiries received • Provide information about digital transformation, IT services • Tell people about the business and what we do • Get customers to contact us and ask questions
CURRENT WEBSITE ANALYSIS Right now, Dube Technologies does not have a dedicated website, which makes it difficult for potential customers to find info about the organization and its services. WEAKNESS IDENTIFIED • No online presence, customers cannot find us on Google • They only rely on word of mouth which limits growth on the company • Customers cannot contact the business online AREAS OF IMPROVEMENT • I can build a website to establish credibility • I can add contact forms and clear service information • The website can be made mobile-friendly so people can access it on any kinds of devices
PROPOSED WEBSITE FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALITY 4.1 The website will have 5 main pages: • Homepage: Here will list at least 3 main services we offer and a Banner • About Us: The background, vision, and mission info will be included • Services: Here we list 8 services with descriptions and icons • Why choose us page: This will include local support, affordable and reliable service • Contact Us page: Phone, email, address, business hours, contact form, and WhatsApp number 4.2 FUNCTIONALITY:
• Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices • Service enquiries and or request forms • Simple Navigation for information to be visible on all pages
DESIGN AND USER EXPERIENCE The website will have a professional, modern, and technology-focused design. The proposed color scheme is dark blue, white, and light grey. Clear headings, simple navigation, and appropriate images will help users find information quickly, which will make the website easy to navigate, readable, and responsive on computers, smartphones, and tablets. TYPOGRAPHY • Heading Font: Arial Bold for clear titles. • Body Font: Arial Regular • Hierarchy: H1 for pages titles, H2 for section titles, P for body text and Line Spacing will be 1.5 LAYOUT AND DESIGN • The Structure will have a Single-column layout with clear sections • Navigation will contain a logo on the left and page links on the right
• The Visual Element will have simple line icons for each of the 8 services offered by the business and a lot of white color will be used so that it looks appealing • Grids will also form part of the website for users that will access the website using Desktops so the max width will be 1200px
WIREFRAMES • Low-fidelity wireframes will be developed to show information such as Home, About, Services, and Contact pages before coding.
TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS HOSTING AND DOMAIN: • Domain name: dubetechnologies.co.za • Web Hosting: Shared hosting plan with1GB storage • Programming Languages: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
EXPECTED OUTCOMES At the end of this project, I will have a responsive and professional 5-page website for Dube Technologies. The website will give users useful information about the business and allow them to contact us or request a query online.
